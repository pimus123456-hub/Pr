Questions academy owners ask

What is AEO and GEO?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO) make your academy easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Gemini to understand, trust and recommend when someone asks where to train.

Is this different from regular SEO?

It builds on SEO. Rankings still matter, but AI assistants answer with a short list of names. We focus on the signals that decide who gets named: clear facts about your programs, structured data, consistent listings, reviews and mentions on sites the assistants already trust.

Which sports academies do you work with?

Training academies and clubs across the US, including baseball, softball, soccer, basketball, volleyball, tennis and multi-sport facilities.

How long until we see results?

Fixes to your site and listings are usually live within the first month. AI assistants refresh what they know on their own schedules, so we track your visibility every month and report what changed.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. We work with your current site, whether it runs on WordPress, Squarespace, Wix or something custom.
